# Current Mile Group One-Page Website Design

## Purpose

Create a restrained public website for Current Mile Group at `currentmile.com`. The site establishes the name, point of view, operating range, and portfolio without implying that the parent entity has already been formed or legally owns the operating companies.

## Structure

The website is a single scrolling page with anchored navigation:

1. Hero
2. Portfolio
3. Sectors
4. Perspective
5. About
6. Contact

There is no blog, separate interior page, contact form, or decorative folio rail.

## Visual Direction

Use the approved Current Mile Group “Datum Line” identity while preserving the selected editorial layout and sharper “Mile Index” voice. The official palette is Ink (`#1A1C19`), Off-White (`#F0ECE3`), Oxide (`#B0552D`), Slate (`#5B6058`), and Bone (`#D8D2C4`). Archivo is used for display, headings, interface text, and running copy; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for captions, figures, and labels. The measured-baseline rule with tick marks indexes content throughout the page. The supplied CMG lockup, monogram, favicon, app icon, and social asset are the source of truth.

The identity should feel grounded, operator-led, premium, durable, and connected to the physical world. Ink and Off-White do nearly all the work. Oxide is punctuation rather than a dominant surface color.

Avoid generic venture-capital aesthetics, synthetic futurism, corporate stock design, ornamental maps, excessive metadata, invented logo symbols, gradients, outlines, shadows, and skewed versions of the mark. Never restyle the portfolio-company logos to match Current Mile Group.

## Content and Claims

- The hero reads: “The next mile is never just distance.”
- The portfolio lists Rangeway, ChargeVia, and AmpIQ by name, with their actual wordmarks and links to their own websites.
- Rangeway is described as EV charging destinations designed around how people actually travel.
- ChargeVia is described as charging infrastructure development for practical deployment.
- AmpIQ is described as independent EV charging consulting, project delivery, and ongoing management.
- The operating range is mobility, energy, infrastructure, software, and hospitality.
- The About section states that the companies operate independently today and that Current Mile Group is intended to become their common parent over time.
- The site does not represent Current Mile Group as already formed, currently owning the portfolio, or participating in Rangeway’s current financing.

## Contact and Footer

Contact is a direct email call to action, not a form. The footer contains only a dynamically generated copyright year and Current Mile Group.

## Behavior and Accessibility

- Header links scroll to their corresponding sections.
- The layout adapts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- All company images and wordmarks include alternative text.
- External portfolio links open in a new tab.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
