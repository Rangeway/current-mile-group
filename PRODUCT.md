# Current Mile Group

## Register

brand

## Users

People looking up Current Mile Group after encountering its name or an email address, including prospective collaborators and operating partners. They need to understand the organization and find its businesses, not read a pitch or submit a form.

## Product Purpose

A public, single-page introduction to Current Mile Group, an active strategic umbrella and intended portfolio parent. Zak confirmed on October 5 that CMG is active and should be described in the present tense. This does not establish legal formation or equity ownership; do not infer either from its operating status. Rangeway and AmpIQ are distinct businesses. ChargeVia by Rangeway is Rangeway's retail host-site charging format, not a third independent company.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, measured, operator-led. Preserve the supplied Datum Line identity and the approved Portfolio Atlas direction with the restraint of Quiet Operator and concrete detail of Built Systems.

## Anti-references

Decorative maps, invented coordinates, pseudo-technical diagrams, generic investment-firm slogans, excessive metadata, filler principles, synthetic-looking corporate imagery, and templated cards. The rejected October preview is an explicit anti-reference.

## Design Principles

1. Explain actual business roles and relationships before making claims about a shared vision.
2. Preserve the portfolio brands' real logos and identities.
3. Use current public websites and maintained operating sources for business descriptions.
4. Keep pipeline, partners, funding, and unformed legal structures out of public copy.
5. Present concept imagery as concepts, not evidence of operating sites.
6. Keep the page concise, with anchored navigation, direct email contact, and a copyright-only footer.
7. Use title case for headings, navigation, calls to action, and standalone labels; use sentence case for body copy and descriptive image alternatives. Preserve official brand styling, including ChargeVia by Rangeway and AmpIQ.
8. Describe CMG at the group level across mobility, energy, infrastructure, software, and hospitality. Those fields can include businesses in their own right; do not limit CMG to EV charging or treat every field as a support function for a charging site. Distinguish this wider remit from the current portfolio without inventing additional operating businesses.
9. CMG’s work is not exclusively internal to the portfolio. Describe the work across all five disciplines without an explicit announcement that CMG is open or willing to work with other companies. Keep the Group description focused on CMG’s work; the Portfolio section already explains the individual brands.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable body type, visible keyboard focus, WCAG AA contrast, descriptive image alternatives, responsive layouts at 360px and above, and respect for reduced motion. Avoid tiny labels.
