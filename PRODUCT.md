# Current Mile Group

## Register

brand

## Users

People looking up Current Mile Group after encountering its name or an email address, including prospective clients, collaborators, and operating partners. They need to understand the organization, its portfolio, and the business-development, project-delivery, and operations work they can engage CMG to do, without submitting a form.

## Product Purpose

A public, single-page introduction to Current Mile Group as the parent group for Rangeway and AmpIQ, with business-development, project-delivery, and operations work across mobility, energy, infrastructure, software, and hospitality. Zak approved the expanded four-paragraph description on October 6. CMG brings the experience behind its businesses together with a network of specialist partners for outside assignments. Do not define CMG as a consulting firm or separate consulting practice. CMG is active and should be described in the present tense; parent-group positioning does not establish legal formation or completed equity transfers. Do not infer either. ChargeVia by Rangeway remains Rangeway's retail host-site charging format, not a third independent company.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, measured, operator-led. Preserve the supplied Datum Line identity and the approved Portfolio Atlas direction with the restraint of Quiet Operator and concrete detail of Built Systems.

## Anti-references

Decorative maps, invented coordinates, pseudo-technical diagrams, generic investment-firm slogans, excessive metadata, filler principles, synthetic-looking corporate imagery, and templated cards. The rejected October preview is an explicit anti-reference.

## Design Principles

1. Explain actual business roles and relationships before making claims about a shared vision.
2. Preserve the portfolio brands' real logos and identities.
3. Use current public websites and maintained operating sources for business descriptions.
4. Keep named or prospective clients, partners, funding, and unformed legal structures out of public copy. The general partner-network delivery model is approved; this does not authorize naming any member or announcing a relationship.
5. Present concept imagery as concepts, not evidence of operating sites.
6. Keep the page concise, with anchored navigation, direct email contact, and a copyright-only footer.
7. Use title case for headings, navigation, calls to action, and standalone labels; use sentence case for body copy and descriptive image alternatives. Preserve official brand styling, including ChargeVia by Rangeway and AmpIQ.
8. Describe CMG at the group level across mobility, energy, infrastructure, software, and hospitality. Those fields can include businesses in their own right; do not limit CMG to EV charging or treat every field as a support function for a charging site. Distinguish this wider remit from the current portfolio without inventing additional operating businesses.
9. Lead with CMG’s parent-group identity, then explain its business-development, project-delivery, and operations work for outside clients. Describe the work across all five disciplines without an explicit announcement that CMG is open or willing to work with other companies. The Portfolio section explains the individual brands; the Group section explains how that experience and the partner network support client engagements.
10. Avoid hypothetical business inventories such as “our interests include” or “the group has room.” Explain what CMG does in active language, and do not repeat the sector list in successive paragraphs or pad the copy to reach a word count.
11. Preserve the approved expanded Group description: parent identity; strategic direction and external work; charging, hospitality, and software scope; and partner coordination and delivery. Removing filler is not authorization to collapse the description into a short summary. Do not reintroduce “consulting” as CMG’s defining label.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable body type, visible keyboard focus, WCAG AA contrast, descriptive image alternatives, responsive layouts at 360px and above, and respect for reduced motion. Avoid tiny labels.
